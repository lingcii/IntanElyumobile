App Overview

Intan Elyu is a gamified tourism companion for La Union featuring smart maps, AI-assisted itinerary planning, AR check-ins, mini-games, and exclusive local vouchers.

🗺️

Explore Map

Discover tourist spots, beaches, and heritage sites with real-time navigation.

📅

Itinerary Planner

Build and save AI-assisted day trips with fare and travel time estimates.

📸

AR Check-In

Scan your surroundings to verify visits and earn XP at destinations.

🎮

GameZone

Play Slide Puzzle, Memory Match, and Word Scramble to earn points.

🏆

Leaderboard

Compete with other tourists across La Union and claim top explorer rank.

🎟️

Vouchers

Redeem XP and Points for exclusive dining, surf, and resort discounts.

📜

Quests

Complete daily, weekly, and monthly challenges to earn bonus rewards.

🌤️

Weather Forecast

Check real-time weather and 5-day forecasts for La Union directly in-app.

Step 1 — Splash Screen

The animated welcome screen that greets you every time you open the app.

1
Open the App
Launch Intan Elyu on your device. The animated splash screen appears with the Intan Elyu logo and tagline "Your Ultimate Travel Companion".
2
Wait for Auto-Detection
The app automatically checks for an active session. If logged in → redirected to Dashboard. If not logged in → transitions smoothly to the Login / Register screen.
The transition animation takes about 2.5 seconds. The logo gracefully morphs into the auth screen for a seamless first impression.

Step 2 — Login & Register

Create your explorer account or sign in to access all app features.

New User — Create an Account

1
Tap the "Register" Tab Required
On the auth screen, tap the Register tab at the top to switch to the registration form.
2
Enter Your Name
Fill in your First Name and Last Name in the two side-by-side input fields.
3
Enter Your Email Address
Type a valid email address — this will be your login credential for all future sessions.
4
Create a Password
Enter a secure password. Tap the eye icon to toggle visibility. The password must meet the app's minimum security requirements.
5
Agree to Terms & Submit
Accept the Terms & Conditions checkbox, then tap the circular arrow (→) button to complete registration. You'll be automatically logged in on success.

Returning User — Login

1
Select the "Login" Tab
The Login tab is selected by default. If not, tap it at the top of the auth screen.
2
Enter Email & Password
Type your registered email address and password. Use the eye icon to reveal your password if needed.
3
Tap the Submit Button
Press the circular arrow (→) button to log in. You will be taken to the Dashboard on success.

Quick Login — Google Sign-In

1
Tap "Sign in with Google" Alternative
Below the login form, tap the Google button. A Google OAuth popup will appear to authorize your account.
2
Choose Your Google Account
Select your Google account. The app will automatically create or retrieve your Intan Elyu account and log you in.

Forgot Password

1
Tap "Forgot Password?"
On the Login form, tap the Forgot Password? link. A password recovery form will slide in.
2
Enter Your Email & Submit
Type your registered email and submit. A password reset link will be sent to your inbox.
3
Open the Reset Link
Open the email from Intan Elyu and tap the reset link. You'll be taken to the password reset page to set a new password.

Step 3 — Dashboard

Your personalized home screen. Monitor explorer stats, live weather, and discover tourist spots.

1
View Your Explorer Profile Card
At the top you'll see your avatar, name, unique Explorer ID, current title (e.g., "Explorer of Elyu"), and your XP level progress bar. Tap the card to view your full profile.
2
Check Your Stats Row
Three quick-stat cards show total Places Visited, total XP Earned, and your current Leaderboard Rank. Tap any stat to navigate to its respective screen.
3
View the Live Weather Widget
A dynamic weather card shows current temperature, conditions, humidity, wind speed, and UV index for San Fernando, La Union. Tap it to open the 5-Day Forecast modal.
4
Access GameZone Panel
Tap the 🧩 GameZone banner to enter the mini-games section where you play for discount vouchers.
5
Browse by Category
Scroll the Categories row horizontally: All Spots, Beach, Mountains, Lakes & Falls, Heritage, Food, Eco-Tourism, and more. Tap a category to filter the tourist spots list below.
6
Explore Tourist Spot Cards
Scroll down to see place cards with photos, names, categories, and municipality info. Tap a card to view full details, ratings, photos, and add it to your itinerary.
7
Use the Bottom Navigation Bar
The bottom nav provides quick access to: Dashboard, Explore Map, Itinerary, Leaderboard, and Profile.

Step 4 — Explore Map

An interactive map of all La Union tourist spots with navigation, fare calculator, and detailed place info.

1
Open the Map Tab
Tap the Map icon in the bottom navigation bar. The interactive map loads centered on La Union, Philippines.
2
Browse Tourist Spot Pins
All registered tourist spots appear as pins on the map. Tap any pin to see a quick preview card with the spot's name, category, municipality, and rating.
3
Use Category Filters
Use the filter chips at the top of the map to narrow pins by category: Beach, Mountains, Heritage, Food, and more.
4
View Full Place Details
Tap a pin then tap "View Details" to open the full place detail sheet. See photos, description, reviews, operating hours, and location info.
5
Calculate Travel Fare
From the detail sheet, tap Fare Calculator. Select your vehicle type (Tricycle, Jeepney, Bus, Taxi, Own Car) and starting point to get an estimated fare and travel time.
6
Add to Itinerary
Tap "Add to Itinerary" from any place detail to include that spot in your current trip plan.
7
Get Navigation Directions
Tap "Navigate" to get turn-by-turn directions from your current GPS location to the selected tourist spot.
Make sure Location Services is enabled in your phone settings for the best map experience.

Step 5 — Itinerary Planner

Plan, save, and manage your day trips to multiple destinations across La Union with route optimization and fare estimates.

1
Open Itinerary
Tap the Route icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Itinerary Planner screen.
2
Create a New Trip
Tap "+ New Trip" or "Plan a Trip". Give your trip a name (e.g., "La Union Beach Day") and set your travel date.
3
Set Your Starting Point
Enter or confirm your starting location (e.g., your hotel or a specific barangay in La Union).
4
Add Destinations
Search for and add tourist spots as stops on your itinerary. You can add multiple places and reorder them via drag-and-drop.
5
Select Transport Mode
Use the route type selector (Tricycle, Jeepney, Bus, etc.) to choose your transport. The app calculates the estimated fare per leg.
6
Review Budget Summary
The Donut Chart and budget breakdown shows your total estimated fare, travel times, and cost distribution across all stops.
7
Save Your Trip
Tap "Save Trip" to save your itinerary. It will be synced to the cloud and accessible in Saved Trips.
8
Execute & Check In Earn XP!
When at a saved trip destination, tap "Check In" next to the stop. Complete the check-in flow to earn +50 XP and +50 PTS per verified visit.

Step 6 — AR Check-In

Verify your presence at a tourist spot using camera and GPS to earn XP and Points.

1
Allow Camera & Location Permissions
When prompted, allow the app to access your camera and GPS location. These are required for check-in verification.
2
Navigate to a Destination
From a saved trip or place card, tap the "Check In" button when you are physically at the location.
3
Point Camera at the Location
The AR camera view opens. A dashed reticle frame appears in the center. Point your camera at the tourist spot or landmark.
4
Wait for Detection
The status indicator at the top shows scan progress. The reticle turns green when the spot is detected and GPS is confirmed.
5
Tap the Scan Button
Press the large circular Scan button at the bottom to capture your check-in photo. The app verifies your GPS location simultaneously.
6
Collect Your Reward XP Earned!
Upon successful verification you receive +50 XP and +50 PTS. A success animation confirms your check-in.
Check-in only works when you are physically present at the tourist spot. GPS verification confirms your proximity.

Step 7 — Quests

Complete daily, weekly, and monthly challenges to earn bonus XP and Points.

1
Open the Quests Page
Navigate to Quests from the Dashboard or side menu to see an overview of active challenges and your progress.
2
Filter by Time Period
Use the time filter pills at the top to switch between Daily, Weekly, and Monthly quests.
3
Review Quest Cards
Each card shows the quest icon, title, difficulty, XP reward, and a progress bar. Completed quests are dimmed with a checkmark.
4
Tap a Quest Card
Tap any quest to view full requirements (e.g., "Visit 3 beaches in San Juan" or "Check in at a heritage site").
5
Complete & Claim Reward
Once all requirements are met, the quest auto-completes and XP is instantly added to your account. Progress is tracked automatically as you use the app.

Step 8 — GameZone

Play three fun mini-games themed around La Union to earn Points redeemable for discount vouchers.

1
Open GameZone
From the Dashboard, tap the 🧩 GameZone banner. Your current Points Balance is shown at the top with a Redeem shortcut.

Game 1 — Slide Puzzle

A
Select "Slide Puzzle" Tab
Tap the Slide Puzzle tab. A scrambled 3×3 image of Tangadan Falls will appear on the board.
B
Rearrange the Tiles
Tap tiles adjacent to the empty space to slide them. Arrange all 8 tiles in the correct order to reveal the complete waterfall image.
C
Solve to Earn Points +100 PTS
Completing the puzzle earns you +100 Points. The app tracks your move count and completion time.

Game 2 — Memory Match

A
Select "Memory Match" Tab
Tap the Memory Match tab. A grid of face-down cards featuring La Union landmark icons appears.
B
Flip & Match Pairs
Tap a card to flip it, then tap another to find its pair. Matched pairs stay face-up; unmatched pairs flip back after a short delay.
C
Match All Pairs to Win +80 PTS
Successfully match all card pairs to earn +80 Points. Fewer attempts = better score!

Game 3 — Word Scramble

A
Select "Word Scramble" Tab
Tap the Word Scramble tab. A scrambled set of letters spelling a La Union–related word appears.
B
Arrange the Letters
Tap the scrambled letters in the correct order to spell out the hidden word (e.g., a tourist spot, local dish, or municipality name).
C
Submit Your Answer +60 PTS
When confident, tap "Submit". Correct answers earn +60 Points per word solved.
Tap the Redeem → button at the top of GameZone to go directly to Discounts & Vouchers to spend your accumulated Points.

Step 9 — Leaderboard

See where you rank among all La Union explorers and compete for the top spot.

1
Open the Leaderboard Tab
Tap the Trophy icon in the bottom navigation bar to open the Leaderboard screen.
2
View Your Current Standing
A personal banner at the top shows your rank, explorer title, and XP. It includes a quick "Explore" button to earn more XP.
3
View the Top 3 Podium
An animated podium displays the Gold 🥇, Silver 🥈, and Bronze 🥉 ranked explorers with avatars, names, and XP totals.
4
Browse the Full Explorer List
Scroll down to see the complete ranked list of all explorers showing rank number, avatar, name, and total XP.
5
Invite Friends to Compete Social
Tap "Invite Friends & Compete" at the bottom to share the leaderboard link via your device's native share sheet.
How to climb ranks: Check in at more tourist spots, complete quests, and play GameZone regularly to accumulate XP and rise to the top.

Step 10 — Discounts & Vouchers

Redeem your earned Points and XP for exclusive deals at local restaurants, surf shops, and resorts.

1
Open Discounts & Vouchers
From the Dashboard or GameZone "Redeem" button, navigate to Discounts & Vouchers. Your current balance is shown at the top.
2
Browse by Category
Filter vouchers: All Deals • 🎟️ My Vouchers • 🍔 Food & Dining • 🏄 Activities & Surf • 🏨 Accommodations • 🎁 Gear & Passes.
3
Select a Voucher
Tap any voucher card to open the Voucher Detail Modal. Review the discount amount, partner business, location, expiration date, and required Points/XP.
4
Tap "Claim Voucher"
Ensure you have enough Points/XP, then tap "Claim Voucher". The cost is deducted from your balance and the voucher is added to My Vouchers.
5
Use Your Voucher at the Business
Visit the partner business and show your voucher code / QR code (accessible from the My Vouchers tab) at the counter to claim your discount.
Check the expiry date of each voucher before claiming. Expired vouchers cannot be reactivated.

Step 11 — My Profile

View explorer stats, badge level, trip history, reviews, and manage your account.

1
Open Your Profile
Tap the Profile icon in the bottom nav, or tap your avatar card on the Dashboard.
2
View Profile Summary Card
See your photo, name, email, bio, and travel preference chips (e.g., Beach Lover, Foodie, Adventurer).
3
Check Your Stats
The Stats Row shows total XP, number of Places Visited, and your current Leaderboard Rank.
4
View Explorer Level Progress
The XP Progress Bar shows your level, accumulated XP, and how much XP you need for the next level.
5
Browse Trip History
Scroll down to Trip History to see all completed visits. Tap "View All" for the full history modal with check-in details and photos.
6
Write Reviews Optional
View testimonials you've written for visited spots or submit a new review from a place's detail page.
7
Edit Your Profile
Tap Edit Profile to update your name, bio, profile photo, and travel preferences. Tap Save Changes to apply.

Step 12 — Settings

Customize app behavior, notifications, privacy, language, and manage your account.

1
Open Settings
From your Profile page, tap the Settings button (gear icon), or navigate from the main menu.
2
Preferences & App Behavior
Toggle the following features using the on/off switches:
  • 🔔 Push Notifications — Alerts for nearby spots & updates
  • 📍 Location Services — High-accuracy GPS for itineraries
  • 🗺️ Offline Maps & Spot Caching — Access info without internet
  • ☁️ Automatic Trip Cloud Sync — Sync itinerary changes live
3
Change App Language Multi-language
Tap the App Language dropdown to select your preferred language. Changes take effect immediately.
4
Privacy & Security
Review data sharing preferences, profile visibility, and linked accounts (e.g., Google).
5
Account Actions
From Settings you can:
  • Change Password — Update your account password
  • Sign Out — Log out from the current device
  • Delete Account — Permanently remove your account
Account Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All XP, vouchers, and trip history will be permanently lost.
